    Maico Freitas/ Hall-Littlewood Polynomials and Hall algebras (1/3).
    Abstract.

    In this seminar, we investigate the isomorphism of algebras between the Hall algebra and the ring of symmetric functions. In particular, we study how the Hall-Littlewood polynomials appear as certain coefficients in this isomorphism.

  • November 8

    Maico Freitas/ Hall-Littlewood Polynomials and Kostka-Foulkes Polynomials. [notes]
    Abstract.

    The Hall-Littlewood functions P_\lambda(x,t) are symmetric functions in variables x_i and coefficients in Z[t] which interpolate between the Schur functions s_\lambda and the monomial symmetric functions m_\lambda, while the Kostka-Foulkes polynomials are the q-analogue of the Kostka numbers. In this seminar, we study the basic properties of the Hall-Littlewood polynomials and the Kostka-Foulkes polynomials.
