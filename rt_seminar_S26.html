  • April 9 (3 pm)

    Andrey Smirnov (UNC Chapel Hill)/ Towards Arithmetic Mirror Symmetry.
    Abstract.

    The theory of p-adic differential equations was initiated by B. Dwork in the 1960s. A distinctive feature of differential equations over p-adic fields is the existence of a Frobenius structure. The characteristic polynomial of the Frobenius automorphism encodes various invariants of the corresponding differential equation. In this talk, I will explore the application of these ideas to quantum differential equations arising in enumerative geometry. In this context, the Frobenius automorphism can be interpreted geometrically, and its characteristic polynomial provides finite-field analogs of mirror symmetry integrals. As a concrete example, I will discuss the application to quantum cohomology of Grassmannians.
